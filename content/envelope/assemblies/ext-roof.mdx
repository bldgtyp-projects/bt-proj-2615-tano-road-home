**Exterior Roof**

The recommended **+R-52 Walls** package uses the **B.Public** R-59 roof panel, delivering an effective whole-assembly
R-58.5. That is already the deepest assembly on the building, and it is the reason further roof insulation returns so
little here.

The **R-80 panel** (effective R-75.8) is carried as a priced option rather than a recommendation. It saves about
0.6 kBtu/ft²·yr relative to the R-59 panel, the smallest return of any upgrade tested here. Two things argue for
taking it anyway if the premium is modest: it is a factory substitution with no detailing consequences (nothing changes
at the window bucks, flashings, wall thickness, or trade sequence), and it is a one-time decision, since the panel is
the roof. Price both panels and take the R-80 if the delta is small.
