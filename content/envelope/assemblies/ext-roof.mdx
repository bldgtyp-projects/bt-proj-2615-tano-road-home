**Exterior Roof**

The **B.Public** R-80 roof panels will work well for this project without additional modification, delivering an
effective whole-assembly R-75.8. The same panel serves both the recommended package and the Phius CORE 2024 case. The
roof is the one assembly that needs no further upgrade to pursue certification.

The R-59 panel (effective R-58.5) is a reasonable alternative if the roof budget is constrained. It costs about
0.6 kBtu/ft²·yr of site energy relative to the R-80 panel, the smallest return of any single upgrade tested in
this report.
