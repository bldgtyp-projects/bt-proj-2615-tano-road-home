**Exterior Roof**

The recommended **+R-52 Walls** package uses the **B.Public** R-59 roof panel, delivering an effective whole-assembly
R-58.5. That is already the deepest assembly on the building, and it is the reason further roof insulation returns so
little here.

The **R-80 panel** (effective R-75.8) is carried as a priced option rather than a recommendation. It saves about
0.6 kBtu/ft²·yr of site energy relative to the R-59 panel, the smallest return of any single upgrade tested in this
report, and roughly 0.16 tons-CO<sub>2e</sub>/yr. Two things argue for taking it anyway if the premium is modest:

- It is a factory panel substitution with no detailing consequences at all. Nothing changes at the window bucks,
  flashings, wall thickness, or trade sequence, which is not true of any other upgrade in this report.
- It is a one-time decision. The panel is the roof, so unlike sub-slab foam or exterior wall insulation there is no
  version of this that gets added later.

Our recommendation is to price both panels and take the R-80 if the delta is small. On energy alone it does not pay
back; on constructability it costs nothing.
