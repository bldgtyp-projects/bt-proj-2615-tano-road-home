**Ground Floor / Slab-on-Grade**

- For the recommended **+R-52 Walls** package, install continuous rigid foam insulation below the concrete slab
  sufficient to achieve an effective **R-25**, roughly 6" of EPS or 5" of XPS.
- For the **Phius CORE 2024** case (illustrated to the left), this increases to an effective **R-40**, or roughly 8" of XPS foam.
- Insulate the slab edge continuously with a vertical thermal break at the perimeter. On a single-storey plan with
  this much perimeter relative to floor area, the slab edge is a disproportionately large loss path, and it is the
  detail most often value-engineered out.
- Provide a continuous capillary break and vapor barrier below the slab, lapped and sealed to the wall air barrier.
