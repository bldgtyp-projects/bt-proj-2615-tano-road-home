**Ground Floor / Slab-on-Grade**

- For the recommended **+R-52 Walls** package, install continuous rigid foam insulation below the concrete slab
  sufficient to achieve an effective **R-25**, roughly 6" of EPS or 5" of XPS.
- For the **Phius CORE 2024** case, this increases to an effective **R-41**, or roughly 10" of EPS. This is one of the
  larger cost deltas between the two packages.
- Insulate the slab edge continuously with a vertical thermal break at the perimeter. On a single-storey plan with
  this much perimeter relative to floor area, the slab edge is a disproportionately large loss path, and it is the
  detail most often value-engineered out.
- Provide a continuous capillary break and vapor barrier below the slab, lapped and sealed to the wall air barrier.

The energy model currently carries thermal bridges as a percentage allowance (10% for the B.Public variants, 2% for the
Phius CORE variant) rather than as calculated perimeter and junction psi-values. This is the main reason this report
does not recommend the certification path: the Phius variant's 3.1% heating-demand margin depends entirely on that 2%
figure holding up under explicit 2D modeling, and on this geometry the slab edge is likely to be among the largest
single contributors. The recommended package carries the far more conservative 10% allowance and does not depend on
that calculation.

That said, the slab-edge thermal break above is worth protecting on its own merits. A single-storey plan with this
much perimeter relative to floor area loses a disproportionate amount through the slab edge whichever package is
built, which makes it one of the details least available for value engineering.
