**Exterior Wall**

For the recommended **+R-52 Walls** package, the **B.Public** R-52 wall panel is used as-is, delivering an effective
whole-assembly R-49.5. No additional exterior insulation is required. This is the single most valuable envelope
upgrade available to the project: relative to the R-35 panel it saves 2.0 kBtu/ft²·yr of site energy, more than three
times the return of the equivalent roof upgrade, and it does so entirely within the factory panel scope.

To reach the **Phius CORE 2024** targets, the exterior walls need to be insulated to an effective R-60 or better. In
that case we assume the same R-52 **B.Public** panel as the base wall, with 3" of continuous exterior mineral wool
added over it, for an effective R-62.1.

- Use [Roxul Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/) or sim.
  with low-conductivity facade clips (plastic or stainless steel).
- Confirm window buck depth, jamb extensions, and flashing details early. Adding 3" of exterior insulation moves the
  window plane outboard and changes nearly every opening detail on the building.
