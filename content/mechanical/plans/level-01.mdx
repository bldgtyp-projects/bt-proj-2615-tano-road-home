Fresh-air ventilation plan showing the supply, extract, and transfer strategy by room. Supply (blue) serves the
bedrooms, living, dining, office, gym, and casita bedroom; extract (red) is drawn from the kitchen, bathrooms, WCs,
laundry, and casita living/kitchen. The garage and storage are outside the thermal envelope and are not ventilated by
this system.
