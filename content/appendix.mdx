### Sources and model provenance

All energy results in this report are drawn from a single PHPP 10.6 workbook (`2615-Tano-Rd-260813.xlsx`). Building
geometry is based on the architectural drawings and models received on
<Var k="source_files.cad_files_received_date" />. Climate data is the
<Var k="narrative.climate.weather_station_name" /> dataset. CO<sub>2e</sub> emission factors are from the
<Var k="narrative.co2.epa_subgrid_name" /> eGRID subregion; source energy factors are from the EPA EnergyStar
Portfolio Manager Technical Reference (2023).

The resilience results come from a separate hourly EnergyPlus model following the Phius REVIVE 2024 protocol, and are
not derived from the PHPP workbook.

### Open items and assumptions to confirm

The following were assumed for modeling purposes and should be confirmed by the design team before these results are
relied on for procurement:

- Window product. Modeled as generic U-0.24 (Improved variant), U-0.22 (B.Public variants, including the recommended
  package) and U-0.15 (Phius CORE variant) installed assemblies with SHGC 0.48. Whole-window performance data for the
  actual specified product, including frame and spacer psi-values, will change these results. The recommended package
  sits exactly on the U-0.22 comfort threshold, so this is the assumption most worth confirming early. See
  [Window Thermal Comfort](/windows/#window-thermal-comfort).
- Thermal bridges. Carried as a percentage allowance (10% for the B.Public variants, 2% for the Phius CORE variant)
  rather than calculated psi-values. The recommended package uses the conservative 10% figure and does not depend on
  this being refined. The Phius heating-demand margin does depend on the 2% figure, so explicit 2D modeling of the
  slab edge, wall-to-roof, and panel-to-panel junctions would be required before that path could be committed to.
- Casita ventilation. Modeled at 25% of design flow when the wing is unoccupied. This assumes zone control on the
  main ERV, which is not yet in the mechanical scope. See
  [Ventilation System Balancing](/mechanical/#ventilation-system-balancing).
- Ventilation duct routing. The Phius CORE variant assumes exterior duct runs are halved and insulation doubled
  relative to the other variants. This needs to be verified as buildable once the ERV location is fixed.
- Heating and cooling equipment. All variants model generic air-source heat pumps for space conditioning and
  domestic hot water. Equipment selection has not yet been made.
- Solar PV. Modeled as zero in all variants. See the
  [Renewable Energy](/energy_model/#renewable-energy) section.
- B.Public wall assembly spec sheets have not yet been received. Only the 59-Roof and 80-Roof sheets are on file. The
  R-52 wall panel is the defining assembly of the recommended package, so its spec sheet is the most important
  outstanding document in this report.

### Units

Passive House modeling is natively metric, and some charts in this report display SI units. Where results are quoted
in the text they have been converted to IP. The two most common conversions used here:

- 1 kWh/m²·yr = 0.317 kBtu/ft²·yr
- 1 W/m² = 0.317 Btu/hr·ft²
