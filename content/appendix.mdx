### Sources and model provenance

All energy results in this report are drawn from a single PHPP 10.6 workbook (`2615-Tano-Rd-260805.xlsx`). Building
geometry is based on the architectural drawings and models received on
<Var k="source_files.cad_files_received_date" />. Climate data is the
<Var k="narrative.climate.weather_station_name" /> dataset. CO<sub>2e</sub> emission factors are from the
<Var k="narrative.co2.epa_subgrid_name" /> eGRID subregion; source energy factors are from the EPA EnergyStar
Portfolio Manager Technical Reference (2023).

The resilience results come from a separate hourly EnergyPlus model following the Phius REVIVE 2024 protocol, and are
not derived from the PHPP workbook.

### Open items and assumptions to confirm

The following were assumed for modeling purposes and should be confirmed by the design team before these results are
relied on for procurement:

- Window product. Modeled as generic U-0.24 (B.Public variants) and U-0.15 (Phius CORE variant) installed
  assemblies with SHGC 0.48. Whole-window performance data for the actual specified product, including frame and
  spacer psi-values, will change these results.
- Thermal bridges. Carried as a percentage allowance (10% / 5%) rather than calculated psi-values. Given this
  building's perimeter and junction count, explicit 2D modeling of the slab edge, wall-to-roof, and panel-to-panel
  junctions is recommended if certification is pursued.
- Ventilation duct routing. The Phius CORE variant assumes exterior duct runs are halved and insulation doubled
  relative to the other variants. This needs to be verified as buildable once the ERV location is fixed.
- Heating and cooling equipment. All variants model generic air-source heat pumps for space conditioning and
  domestic hot water. Equipment selection has not yet been made.
- Solar PV. Modeled as zero in all variants. See the
  [Renewable Energy](/energy_model/#renewable-energy) section.
- B.Public wall assembly spec sheets have not yet been received. Only the 59-Roof and 80-Roof sheets are on file.

### Units

Passive House modeling is natively metric, and some charts in this report display SI units. Where results are quoted
in the text they have been converted to IP. The two most common conversions used here:

- 1 kWh/m²·yr = 0.317 kBtu/ft²·yr
- 1 W/m² = 0.317 Btu/hr·ft²
