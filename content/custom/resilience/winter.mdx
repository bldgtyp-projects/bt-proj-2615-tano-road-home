For assessing the winter resiliency, the **Phius REVIVE** protocol uses the [Standard Effective Temperature (SET)](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/9-5/engineering-reference/resilience-metrics.html#set-hour) metric 
in order to evaluate zone habitability during the stress-period. In particular, to comply with the **Phius REVIVE** standard, 
it is required that all zones exhibit:

- 0 hours below 2 °C [36°F] **SET**
- < 216 °F-Hours below 12 °C [54°F] **SET**

<div class="btwr-callout">
  <strong>Result</strong>
  <span>
    Both criteria are met with a wide margin. Every zone records **0 hours** below 2 °C **SET**, and **0 °F-Hours** 
    below 12 °C [54 °F] **SET**, against the 216 °F-Hour allowance. The minimum **SET** reached in any zone over the 
    seven-day outage is approximately 15.1 °C [59.2 °F]. Thanks to the well-insulated envelope, high-quality glazing, 
    and good airtightness, interior dry-bulb temperature falls from the 20 °C [68 °F] setpoint to a low of only 
    10.8 °C [51.4 °F] by the seventh day, even as outdoor air drops to -20.0 °C [-4.0 °F]. The 
    temperature decline is also not monotonic: solar gain through the south glazing partially re-warms the home each day, 
    which is what holds the zones above the threshold late in the event. Based on these results, no modifications are 
    recommended at this time.
  </span>
</div>
