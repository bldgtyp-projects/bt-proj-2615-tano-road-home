As shown in the results below, in order to assess the building performance we have tested the building in six distinct
configurations (variants). Variants 3 through 5 are cumulative: each one carries forward the improvements of the one
before it.

1. **Code-Minimum:** A version which meets but does not exceed the **<VarLink
hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.code_base_url" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var
k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> / <Var k="narrative.energy_code.zone" /></VarLink>** code minimums for assembly
R-values, window U-values, airtightness and equipment efficiencies. This variant assumes a typical residential
"extract-only" (bath fan) ventilation system, and that the building uses all-electric appliances and equipment for
heating, cooling, hot-water and cooking. Modeled at R-10 sub-slab, U-0.32 windows, and 3.0 ACH50.

2. **Improved:** This variant improves the window U-values, assembly R-values, and total airtightness of the
building beyond the "code-minimum" levels, but not quite to full "Passive House" level. Windows improve to U-0.24,
airtightness to 1.0 ACH50, and the sub-slab insulation to R-25. This variant also introduces a dedicated balanced
fresh-air ventilation system with heat and moisture recovery (ERV), modeled at 75% effective heat recovery and 68%
moisture recovery.

3. **R-35 Walls / R-59 Roof:** A variant which uses the **B.Public** wall (R-35) and roof (R-59) panel system for
the envelope of the home. As modeled, these panels deliver an effective whole-assembly R-33.5 at the walls and R-58.5
at the roof. This variant and the two above it also improve the windows slightly, to an installed U-0.22, which is the
level Passive House comfort guidance recommends for this climate. All other inputs match the "Improved" case.

4. **+R-52 Walls (recommended):** A variant which upgrades all wall surfaces to the **B.Public** R-52 wall panel option
(effective R-49.5). This is the package recommended in this report: it captures the last steep return on the insulation
curve while staying within a single factory-panel scope.

5. **+R-80 Roof:** A variant which also upgrades all roof surfaces to the **B.Public** R-80 roof panel option, the 
full B.Public package. Carried as a priced option rather than a recommendation: the roof is
already the best-insulated assembly, so this step returns less energy than any other upgrade tested.

6. **Phius CORE 2024:** A version configured to pursue the rigorous **Phius CORE 2024** certification standard. On top
of the full **B.Public** package this variant adds 3" of continuous exterior mineral wool at the walls,
raises the sub-slab insulation to R-40, upgrades the windows from U-0.22 to U-0.15 triple glazing, tightens the thermal-bridge
allowance from 10% to 2%, and shortens the exterior ventilation duct runs while doubling their insulation thickness.

<div class="btwr-callout">
  <strong>Note on airtightness</strong>
  <span>
    Variants 2 through 5 are all modeled at 1.0 ACH50 and variant 6 at 0.6 ACH50. As discussed in the
    <a href="/building_envelope/#building-airtightness">Building Airtightness</a> section, 1.0 ACH50 already
    clears the Phius area-based airtightness limit for this building by a wide margin, so the difference between
    those two numbers is a small energy refinement rather than a certification requirement.
  </span>
</div>
